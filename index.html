Who We Are

Boda boda motorcycle taxis move people and goods through Uganda's towns, trading centres and villages, often reaching places no other transport can. Behind every rider is a stage — a local parking and pick-up point where riders gather, look out for one another and organise themselves.

The Boda Boda Stage Association brings these stages together. By organising under one umbrella, riders gain a stronger voice with local authorities, better access to training and savings, and a shared standard of professionalism.

What We Do

Our work focuses on the everyday needs of riders and the communities they serve.

🪖

Road Safety

Promoting helmet use for rider and passenger, traffic-rule awareness, and safe riding practices.

📋

Registration & Licensing

Helping members register their stage, obtain valid permits and driving licences, and keep documents in order.

💰

Savings & Credit

Encouraging group savings and SACCO membership so riders can build capital, insure their bikes and plan ahead.

🤝

Welfare Support

Rallying members during accidents, illness, bereavement and other emergencies.

🏛️

Representation

Speaking with one voice to city, municipal and district authorities, police and other partners.

🎓

Training

Customer care, first aid, bike maintenance and financial literacy workshops for riders.

How a Stage Is Organised

Each stage runs on clear roles and shared rules, so that every member is accountable.

Chairperson

Leads the stage, represents it to authorities and settles disputes.

Secretary

Keeps the member register, minutes and official correspondence.

Treasurer

Looks after stage contributions and savings transparently.

Welfare Officer

Coordinates support for members in need.

Discipline Officer

Upholds the code of conduct and resolves complaints.

Our Code of Conduct

Respect on the road builds trust in every community we serve.

  1. Always wear a helmet and provide one for your passenger.
  2. Ride sober and rested. Never ride under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
  3. Carry valid documents: licence, permit and proof of stage membership.
  4. Obey traffic rules. No overloading, no red-light running, no wrong-way riding.
  5. Treat every passenger with respect, especially women, children and the elderly.
  6. Charge fair fares and agree on the price before the journey.
  7. Support your fellow riders and keep your stage clean and orderly.
